# FocusGuard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

FocusGuard ("the app") is an Android app that blocks distracting apps during focus sessions and when
daily time limits are reached, and shows how much time you spend in each app. This policy explains what
the app does with information. In short: **FocusGuard does not collect, transmit or share your data.**

## Information the app uses

| Information | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **App usage statistics** (which app was in the foreground and for how long), read through Android's Usage Access permission | To show your screen-time overview and to enforce daily limits | Processed on your device only |
| **The name of the app that comes to the foreground**, read through Android's Accessibility Service | To show the block screen when an app you chose to block is opened | Processed on your device only; never stored as a history |
| **The list of apps installed on your device** (apps with a launcher icon) | To let you choose which apps to block and to show app names and icons | Stays on your device |
| **Your focus sessions, daily limits and settings** (names, times, chosen apps, theme) | So the app can remember your configuration | Stored in the app's private storage on your device |

## Accessibility Service

FocusGuard uses Android's Accessibility Service only to detect which app has just opened so it can display
its block screen. It does **not** read the content of your screen, the text you type, passwords, messages
or notifications, and it does not perform taps, gestures or changes to your settings. It never prevents you
from disabling or uninstalling the app. The service is optional and can be turned off at any time in
Android Settings > Accessibility; without it, blocking does not work. Before you are sent to those settings,
the app shows a disclosure and asks for your consent.

## Data collection, sharing and security

- The app has **no internet permission** and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting
  libraries. It does not send information to the developer or to any third party.
- Your configuration is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a
  non-rooted device.
- If device backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your Google backup
  according to your device settings; this is handled by Android, not by the app.
- Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
